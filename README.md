# Shishō 師匠

Find your shishō. Follow a strategy you can check.

A strategy marketplace for RYO-CHAN: traders publish their strategy as exact rules, an agent runs every rule hourly on RYO-CHAN's live research tools and records each practice trade with the evidence behind it, anyone can replay any decision from that evidence, and people follow the shishō whose record they trust.

RYO-CHAN Hackathon 2026 · Track 1 (Autonomous Agents) + Track 2 (Dashboards & Interfaces). Full documentation to follow.
